## 3. Applied corrections

- **END wording (1).** Shared `bye` text is now "Thank you. This enquiry is complete." The earlier "You can message us again any time" is withdrawn: after a session ends a 30-minute cooldown silently ignores a new start (and 3 sessions per 24 h are allowed).
- **Registration cancel wording (2).** Replaces "Nothing has been sent." New text: "No problem. I have not sent your details to our staff for follow-up. Messages you have already sent in this chat remain in the conversation." (Applies when 08G-5 is implemented.)
- **Free-text cancellation (3).** A `QUESTION` node accepts any text within its bounds as the answer; the engine does not interpret words such as "cancel" or "stop". Cancellation is offered only at `BUTTONS` or `LIST` nodes (for Registration: consent and confirm). A student who wants to stop at a free-text question stops replying (idle timeout completes the session silently) or sends a message staff will see after handover. This is a runtime property, not changed here.
- **Scan definition (4).** A forbidden-claim check examines one sentence at a time. A sentence is a **false claim** when it matches a claim pattern (payment received/confirmed/verified; registration or enrolment confirmed/complete/successful; "you are registered/enrolled") and contains **no negation** (not, no, never, can't, cannot, isn't, haven't, hasn't, don't, won't). A negated sentence ("This is not a confirmed registration", "I can't confirm payments") is a **safe negation** and passes. The scanner itself is unit-tested with both kinds. The scan covers student-facing text only (bodies, titles, descriptions, messages), never identifiers.
